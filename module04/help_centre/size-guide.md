# Size guide

## Footwear sizes
Footwear is sold in UK sizes. If you are between sizes, choose the larger one. As a guide, UK 7 fits a foot length of about 25.4 cm, UK 8 about 26.2 cm, UK 9 about 27.1 cm and UK 10 about 27.9 cm.

## Clothing sizes
Clothing chest sizes: S fits 36-38 inches, M fits 38-40, L fits 40-42, XL fits 42-44 and XXL fits 44-46. Each product page also has its own measurement chart.
