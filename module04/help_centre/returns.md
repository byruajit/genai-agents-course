# Returns policy

## Return windows
You can return most items within 10 days of delivery. Damaged, defective or wrong items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The window is counted in calendar days from the delivery date shown in My Orders.

## Items that cannot be returned
Innerwear, groceries and gift cards are non-returnable, even if unopened, for hygiene and safety reasons. If one of these arrives damaged or is the wrong item, contact support within 30 days and we will help you directly.

## Condition of returned items
Returned items must be unused, with original tags, packaging and any free gifts that came with them. Footwear must be returned in the original shoe box. Items returned used, washed or without tags may be sent back to you without a refund.

## How to start a return
Go to My Orders, choose the order and select Return. Pick a reason and a pickup slot. A courier usually picks the item up within 2 working days. You will get an SMS and email when the pickup is scheduled and when it is done.

## When pickup is not available
A few remote pincodes do not have return pickup. In that case My Orders will ask you to ship the item yourself to our returns centre; we reimburse up to Rs 100 of courier charges as store credit once the item reaches us.
