# Warranty

## Warranty periods
Electronics sold by Northwind come with the manufacturer's warranty: 1 year for speakers, headphones, earbuds and fitness bands, and 6 months for power banks and computer accessories. Kitchen appliances such as kettles have a 1-year warranty. Footwear, clothing and bags have no warranty but are covered by the 30-day return window for defects.

## Return or warranty?
In the first 30 days after delivery, a defective item can simply be returned. After 30 days, a defect is handled as a warranty claim instead of a return.

## How to make a warranty claim
Go to My Orders, open the order and choose Warranty. Describe the problem and upload a photo or short video. We arrange a pickup for repair or replacement, usually within 7-10 working days. Keep the invoice from My Orders - the warranty runs from the delivery date.

## What the warranty does not cover
Physical damage, water damage beyond the product's rating, normal wear of batteries and ear tips, and repairs by unauthorised service centres are not covered.
