# Contacting customer support

## Support hours
Customer support is available 9am-9pm, every day, including Sundays and public holidays.

## Ways to reach us
Chat with ShopAssist at any time. To talk to a person, ask ShopAssist to raise a support ticket during support hours, or email support@northwind.example. A support agent replies to tickets within 24 hours.

## Escalations
If your issue is not solved by a support agent within 3 days, reply to the ticket email with the word ESCALATE and a senior agent will take it over.
