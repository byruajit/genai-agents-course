# Payment methods

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, Northwind store credit and cash on delivery.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available on orders up to Rs 10,000 at serviceable pincodes, with no extra fee. You can pay the courier in cash or by scanning their UPI QR code. Gift cards cannot be bought with cash on delivery.

## No-cost EMI
No-cost EMI for 3 or 6 months is available on orders above Rs 3,000 with credit cards from HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and SBI Card. The interest is given back as an instant discount. Your bank may charge a processing fee.

## Failed payments
If money was debited but the order was not placed, it is automatically reversed to your account within 5-7 working days. You do not need to contact us.

## Payment safety
Northwind will never ask for your card number, CVV, OTP, UPI PIN or password - not in chat, email or on a call.
