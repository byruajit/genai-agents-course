# Damaged, defective or wrong items

## Reporting a problem
If an item arrives damaged, does not work, or is not what you ordered, report it in My Orders within 30 days of delivery by choosing Return with the reason damaged, defective or wrong item.

## Photos help
Add clear photos of the item, the label and the outer box. For electronics, a short unboxing video speeds things up. Keep the original packaging until the return is picked up.

## Replacement or refund
You can choose a replacement, if the item is in stock, or a refund. Replacements ship within 2 working days of the pickup.

## Tampered packages
If the outer package looks opened or tampered with at delivery, do not accept it - refuse the delivery and we will refund or reship.
