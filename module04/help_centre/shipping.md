# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery charges
Standard delivery is free on orders above Rs 499 and costs Rs 49 below that. Express delivery costs Rs 99 on any order.

## Delivery times
Standard delivery to metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata) takes 2-3 working days; other pincodes take 4-6 working days. Express delivery is next day in metro cities and 2-3 working days elsewhere. Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

## Where we deliver
We deliver only within India, to serviceable pincodes. We do not ship internationally. You can check a pincode on any product page before ordering.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier tries to deliver up to 3 times and calls you before each attempt. After 3 failed attempts the order returns to us; prepaid orders are then refunded in full.

## Tracking
Every shipped order gets a tracking link by SMS and email. You can also see the status in My Orders: placed, shipped, out for delivery, delivered or cancelled.
