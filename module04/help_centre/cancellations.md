# Cancelling an order

## Before the order ships
You can cancel an order free of charge on the website, in My Orders, until it ships. Prepaid orders are refunded in full within 5-7 working days.

## After the order has shipped
A shipped order cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery when the courier arrives, or accept it and return it if the item is returnable.

## Partial cancellation
In an order with several items you can cancel individual items before they ship. Each item ships separately, so some items may already be on their way.

## Cash-on-delivery cancellations
If several cash-on-delivery orders are refused at the door, cash on delivery may be switched off for your account for 90 days. Prepaid payment options stay available.
