# Refunds

## Refund timeline
Refunds go to the original payment method within 5-7 working days after the return is picked up. Your bank may take a little longer to show it on your statement.

## Cash-on-delivery refunds
Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account. Add the account on the website under My Orders > Refund details. For your safety, never share bank details in chat, email or on a phone call.

## Store credit option
Instead of a refund to the original payment method, you can choose Northwind store credit when you start the return. Store credit is added within 24 hours of pickup and can be used on any future order. It does not expire for 12 months.

## Partial refunds and coupons
If you used a coupon and return only part of an order, the coupon discount is split across the items, so the refund for the returned item is its price minus its share of the discount. Delivery charges are refunded only if the whole order is returned because it was damaged, defective or the wrong item.

## Refund for an undelivered order
If a prepaid order is cancelled before it ships, or comes back to us undelivered after 3 failed delivery attempts, the full amount including delivery charges is refunded within 5-7 working days.
