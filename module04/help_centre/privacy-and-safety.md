# Privacy and safety

## What we never ask for
Northwind will never ask for your OTP, CVV, card number, UPI PIN, password or bank login in chat, by email, SMS or phone. Anyone who asks is not from Northwind.

## Fake calls and messages
Fraudsters sometimes call pretending to be from a courier or from Northwind and offer a refund in exchange for a code. Do not share the code. Report it by raising a support ticket.

## Your data
We use your name, address and phone number only to deliver orders and provide support. Chat conversations are kept for 30 days to improve support and are then deleted.
