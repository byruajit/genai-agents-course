# Gift cards

## Using a gift card
Northwind gift cards are delivered by email and can be redeemed at checkout. You can combine a gift card with another payment method if the order costs more than the card balance.

## Validity
Gift cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. The balance cannot be extended after expiry.

## Restrictions
Gift cards are non-returnable, cannot be cancelled once delivered, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be bought with cash on delivery or with another gift card.
