# Account and delivery address

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address of an order in My Orders until it ships. After an order has shipped the address cannot be changed; you can refuse the delivery and order again to the new address.

## Saved addresses
You can save up to 10 addresses in your account under Settings > Addresses.

## Changing your phone number or email
Update your phone number or email under Settings > Profile. We verify the new number or email with a one-time code that only you should see - never share it with anyone, including Northwind staff.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion under Settings > Privacy. Open orders and returns must be completed first. Deletion is permanent after 30 days.
